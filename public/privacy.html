The information collected by log files includes IP addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable and are used for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement, and gathering demographic information.

Cookies

Like many websites, we use cookies to store certain preferences (such as theme) and your optional display name. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. Note that some features may not function properly without cookies.

Local Storage and IndexedDB

The game stores settings and gameplay statistics locally in your browser using local storage and IndexedDB. This data stays on your device unless you clear your browser data.
